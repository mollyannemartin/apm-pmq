# APM source integration

The revision site includes a paraphrased study map based on:
- APM Project Management Qualification Handbook (uploaded 2026-10-08; document copyright 2024)
- APM Project Management Qualification Sample Paper (document copyright 2024)
- APM Project Management Qualification Sample Paper 2 (document copyright 2026)

The site does not reproduce the official papers. The new Read-page section summarises the exam structure,
coverage, and recurring skills/themes to help Molly revise before receiving her course workbook.

When the workbook and instructor material are received, those materials should take priority over this
study aid and any conflicting wording should be updated accordingly.
